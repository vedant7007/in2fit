## The threshold, written first

1. WRONG VALUE = 0 and WRONG RANGE = 0 on every fixture, rendered or photographed; one
   misread number is a stop. A range read as none is silence (no rule fires), a miss, scored
   apart from a range read wrong.
2. On the rendered sheets, nothing missed outside the two known ceilings (two-column,
   range-before-result), which may miss and may not misread.
3. On at least three photographs of the demo's printed sheet (square-on, about 20 degrees off,
   warm indoor light): fasting glucose and HbA1c read with value and printed range on all three,
   and the date on the square shot.

All three hold: Beat 3 is safe to perform live. Only the angled shot misses a demo row: safe
square-on, S holds the sheet flat, the run of show says so. A photograph reads a wrong number
or misses both demo rows: not safe; the presenter opens the pre-saved report (run of show,
item 7). OCR time per sheet is reported, not gated.
