# IN2FIT — help us test the voice recogniser (10 minutes)

We are building an app that lets you say what you ate, in Telugu, Hindi or English, or all
three mixed together, the way people actually talk. To check it hears real people, we need
real voices. Your recordings are used only to test the app's speech recognition. They are not
published, not used to train anything, and deleted if you ask.

## What to do

1. Open the **voice recorder app already on your phone** (Samsung "Voice Recorder", Google
   "Recorder", realme/OPPO "Recorder", iPhone "Voice Memos" — any of them is fine).
2. Sit somewhere ordinary. A kitchen or a room with a fan is fine. Don't hunt for silence.
3. Hold the phone the way you hold it for a normal call on speaker, about a hand's length away.
4. **One recording per sentence.** Tap record, wait a second, say the sentence once the way you
   would say it to a friend, wait a second, stop.
5. **Do not re-record** because you paused, said "umm", or corrected yourself. That is exactly
   what we need to hear. Re-record only if you coughed or someone shouted.
6. Name each file like this, then send all 13 back in the same chat you got this from:

       yourname_te_01.m4a      yourname_te_02.m4a      ...      yourname_en_13.m4a

   Use the language letters printed next to each sentence (te / hi / en). Whatever format your
   recorder saves (m4a, aac, mp3, wav, 3gp) is fine. Don't convert anything.

7. Reply with one line: your first language, your age (roughly), and the town you grew up in.

## The 13 sentences

Read the Telugu and Hindi ones in your own accent. Where English words sit inside a Telugu or
Hindi sentence, say them the way you normally would, not in an "English" voice.

**Telugu**

1. `te` — నేను రెండు రొట్టెలు మరియు పప్పు తిన్నాను
   *(nēnu reṇḍu roṭṭelu mariyu pappu tinnānu)*
2. `te` — ఉదయం మూడు ఇడ్లీ, సాంబార్, కొబ్బరి పచ్చడి తిన్నాను
   *(udayaṁ mūḍu iḍlī, sāmbār, kobbari pachchaḍi tinnānu)*
3. `te` — రాత్రి two rotis, dal fry, one glass milk తీసుకున్నాను
   *(rātri two rotis, dal fry, one glass milk tīsukunnānu)*
4. `te` — నాకు షుగర్ ఉంది, రాత్రి ఏం తినాలి?
   *(nāku shugar undi, rātri ēṁ tinālī?)*
5. `te` — నేను ఒకటి... కాదు, రెండు దోసెలు తిన్నాను, పచ్చడితో
   *(nēnu okaṭi... kādu, reṇḍu dōselu tinnānu, pachchaḍitō — yes, with the pause and the correction)*

**Hindi**

6. `hi` — मैंने दो रोटी, दाल और थोड़ा चावल खाया
7. `hi` — सुबह one glass milk और दो boiled eggs लिए
8. `hi` — मुझे iron कम है, क्या खाना चाहिए?

**English (your normal Indian English)**

9. `en` — For lunch I had two rotis, some pappu and a katori of curd.
10. `en` — I drank 200 ml of milk and ate one boiled egg.
11. `en` — I have anaemia, what should I eat for iron?

**Your own words** (any language, or mixed — however you really talk)

12. Say what you ate for dinner yesterday. Name the file with the language you mostly used.
13. Ask the app one question about your food or your health, the way you'd ask a friend.

That's it. Thank you — five minutes of your voice is worth more to this than a week of our
guessing.
