# Devanagari words in the routing lists: for Vedant to check

Every Devanagari word below was GENERATED by a session that does not read Hindi, on 20 September
2026 evening, so that the Hindi recogniser's output (which writes Devanagari) reaches the same
rules as the English. Vedant reads Devanagari; this is his five-minute check, not a reviewer's
evening. Per Meera's 18:07 finding the demo stands on English; these are insurance.

For each word: does it mean what the list says? Could it mean something else a person would say
in the other kind of sentence? Strike what is wrong; the list is in the file named.

What each list DOES, so the cost of a wrong entry is clear:

- a MARKER or a CLINICAL word anywhere in the sentence stops the app writing a meal (safe direction);
- a LOG WORD or a QUANTITY WORD, with no marker, makes the app write a meal (the dangerous direction);
- a NEGATION stops the app writing a meal even when a log or quantity word is present;
- HISTORY / PLATE_NOW / RECOMMEND phrases decide which answer path runs, without the model;
- a nutrient word is read as its English tag when the knowledge file is searched.
